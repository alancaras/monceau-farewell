Monceau has stopped brewing. After five years of natural, funky fermentation, this page is our thank you and farewell.

What this was

A small Melbourne brewery making pét‑nat kombucha, a sparkling non‑alc wine alternative and a non‑alc pils, with real ingredients and nothing you couldn’t pronounce.

We started Monceau in 2020 because we wanted to drink less, without drinking worse. The first bottles were pét‑nat kombucha, fermented and carbonated the ancestral way, with Fuji apple juice from West Gippsland and blood orange from the Riverland. No two bottles were quite the same.

From there it grew into a brewery in Brunswick, a range of fruit, a sparkling non‑alc vermentino called Monte Stella, a non‑alc pils called Parc, and a fridge in more than six hundred bars, restaurants, wine stores and grocers around Australia. Sommeliers poured it. Picnic blankets hosted it. Many dinner parties were, we’re told, not embarrassed by it.

Now it’s time to put the last bottle in the fridge. Some of the drinks we created found new homes. Monceau itself has said farewell.

Thank you to everyone who brewed, bottled, labelled, delivered, stocked and poured it. Thank you to the chefs and sommeliers who took a punt on a kombucha with a wine label. And thank you most of all to everyone who bought a bottle, popped it open, and shared it with someone.

What we made

real ingredients. real flavour.

Everything was fermented, never force‑carbonated or sweetened, and the full ingredients list was always on the bottle.

  • Pét‑nat kombucha

    Our first drink and our favourite. Tea, sugar, culture, then fruit juice and a second ferment in the bottle. Just over 1% ABV, dry and funky. 330ml and 750ml.

    Classic: Fuji Apple · Blood Orange · Pear · Mandarin
    Editions: Granny Smith · Plum
    Pure: Cerise · Grapefruit · Passionfruit

  • Monte Stella

    A sparkling non‑alc wine alternative made from wine grapes. Our hybrid ferment, short-lived but well loved.

    Vermentino · Negroamaro · Roussanne

  • Parc Pils

    A crisp non‑alc pilsner for the end of a hot day. Brewed in Brunswick, drunk mostly in parks, as intended.

    Non‑alc beer · 330ml
